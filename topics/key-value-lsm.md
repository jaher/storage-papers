# Key-Value Stores & LSM Trees

Key-value stores and LSM-trees: Dynamo, Bigtable, Cassandra, LevelDB/RocksDB lineage, learned indexes, and compaction design.

| Paper | Importance | arXiv | Authors | Published | Added | Summary |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| The Log-Structured Merge-Tree (LSM-Tree) | ★★★★★ | https://doi.org/10.1007/s002360050048 | O'Neil et al. | 1996-06-01 | 2026-11-06 | Introduced the LSM-tree: batched, merge-sort-like cascading of inserts from a memory component through disk components for low-cost indexing of high-insert workloads. |
| Bigtable: A Distributed Storage System for Structured Data | ★★★★★ | https://www.usenix.org/legacy/event/osdi06/tech/chang/chang_html/ | Chang et al. | 2006-11-01 | 2026-11-06 | Google's petabyte-scale sparse multidimensional sorted map over GFS/Chubby, the canonical distributed LSM/SSTable design that HBase and Cassandra descend from. |
| Dynamo: Amazon's Highly Available Key-Value Store | ★★★★★ | https://www.allthingsdistributed.com/files/amazon-dynamo-sosp2007.pdf | DeCandia et al. | 2007-10-14 | 2026-11-06 | Amazon's always-on KV store combining consistent hashing, vector clocks, quorum reads/writes and anti-entropy, the canonical eventually-consistent NoSQL reference. |
| LevelDB | ★★★★★ | https://github.com/google/leveldb | Ghemawat et al. | 2011-05-01 | 2026-11-06 | Google's embedded LSM key-value library by Dean and Ghemawat, the Bigtable tablet stack reimplemented for open source; no peer-reviewed paper exists, so the repo is the primary source. |
| The Case for Learned Index Structures | ★★★★★ | [1712.01208](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.01208) | Kraska et al. | 2018-06-01 | 2026-11-06 | Reframed indexes as learned models predicting key positions, launching the learned-index field that Bourbon and ALEX later applied to LSM-trees. |
| FAWN: A Fast Array of Wimpy Nodes | ★★★★☆ | http://phanishayee.me/papers/2009_SOSP_FAWN.pdf | Andersen et al. | 2009-10-01 | 2026-11-06 | Built FAWN-KV, a replicated KV store on low-power nodes with flash and a log-structured per-node store, achieving ~350 queries per joule (SOSP best paper). |
| Cassandra: A Decentralized Structured Storage System | ★★★★☆ | https://www.cs.cornell.edu/projects/ladis2009/papers/lakshman-ladis2009.pdf | Lakshman et al. | 2010-04-01 | 2026-11-06 | Facebook's synthesis of Dynamo's partitioning/replication with Bigtable's data model and LSM storage, built for Inbox Search at hundreds of nodes. |
| SILT: A Memory-Efficient, High-Performance Key-Value Store | ★★★★☆ | https://www.usenix.org/legacy/event/atc11/tech/final_files/Lim.pdf | Lim et al. | 2011-10-01 | 2026-11-06 | Combined Log, Hash and Sorted stores with compressed indexes to serve billions of entries at 0.7 bytes of DRAM per entry and 1.01 flash reads per lookup. |
| Cache Craftiness for Fast Multicore Key-Value Storage (Masstree) | ★★★★☆ | https://pdos.csail.mit.edu/papers/masstree:eurosys12.pdf | Mao et al. | 2012-04-01 | 2026-11-06 | In-memory Masstree, a trie of B+-trees with cache-conscious layout and versioned concurrency, reaching 6–10M ops/sec on 16 cores. |
| WiscKey: Separating Keys from Values in SSD-Conscious Storage | ★★★★☆ | https://www.usenix.org/conference/fast16/technical-sessions/presentation/lu | Lu et al. | 2016-02-01 | 2026-11-06 | Separated keys (kept in the LSM-tree) from values (append-only log), cutting write amplification and beating LevelDB by 2.5–111× on loads. |
| Optimizing Space Amplification in RocksDB | ★★★★☆ | https://www.michaelstumm.com/Papers/Dong-CIDR-16.pdf | Dong et al. | 2017-01-01 | 2026-11-06 | Meta's account of tuning RocksDB in production (dynamic level sizing, tiered compression), halving MySQL storage vs InnoDB while meeting latency SLOs. |
| Monkey: Optimal Navigable Key-Value Store | ★★★★☆ | https://doi.org/10.1145/3035918.3064054 | Dayan et al. | 2017-05-01 | 2026-11-06 | Showed lookup cost is the sum of Bloom-filter false-positive rates across levels and allocated filter memory optimally across levels, cutting lookup latency 50–80%. |
| ALEX: An Updatable Adaptive Learned Index | ★★★★☆ | [1905.08898](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.08898) | Ding et al. | 2020-06-01 | 2026-11-06 | First practical updatable learned index, using model-based inserts into gapped arrays to beat B+-trees by up to 4.1× on read-write workloads. |
| MyRocks: LSM-Tree Database Storage Engine Serving Facebook's Social Graph | ★★★★☆ | https://doi.org/10.14778/3415478.3415546 | Matsunobu et al. | 2020-08-01 | 2026-11-06 | Documents Facebook's migration of its tens-of-petabytes User Database from InnoDB to RocksDB-backed MyRocks, cutting instance size 62.3%. |
| bLSM: A General Purpose Log Structured Merge Tree | ★★★☆☆ | https://doi.org/10.1145/2213836.2213862 | Sears et al. | 2012-05-01 | 2026-11-06 | Added B-tree-like read/scan performance and a "spring and gear" merge scheduler that bounds write latency to an LSM-tree. |
| HyperDex: A Distributed, Searchable Key-Value Store | ★★★☆☆ | https://www.usenix.org/system/files/login/articles/escriva12-06.pdf | Escriva et al. | 2012-08-01 | 2026-11-06 | Distributed KV store with hyperspace hashing for secondary-attribute search; its storage engine was HyperLevelDB, the LevelDB fork this row covers. |
| Scaling Concurrent Log-Structured Data Stores (cLSM) | ★★★☆☆ | https://readingxtra.github.io/docs/a32-golan-gueta.pdf | Golan-Gueta et al. | 2015-04-01 | 2026-11-06 | Replaced LevelDB's in-memory component with a non-blocking concurrent map plus snapshot scans and atomic read-modify-write, improving throughput 1.5–2.5×. |
| Designing Access Methods: The RUM Conjecture | ★★★☆☆ | https://openproceedings.org/2016/conf/edbt/paper-12.pdf | Athanassoulis et al. | 2016-03-01 | 2026-11-06 | Conjectured that no access method can simultaneously optimise read, update and memory overhead, framing the LSM design space Monkey and Dostoevsky later navigated. |
| BadgerDB | ★★★☆☆ | https://github.com/dgraph-io/badger | Jain et al. | 2017-01-01 | 2026-11-06 | Pure-Go embedded KV store for Dgraph implementing WiscKey-style key-value separation (LSM tree for keys, value log for values); no peer-reviewed paper exists. |
| PebblesDB: Building Key-Value Stores using Fragmented Log-Structured Merge Trees | ★★★☆☆ | https://doi.org/10.1145/3132747.3132765 | Raju et al. | 2017-10-01 | 2026-11-06 | Introduced Fragmented LSM-trees with guards to avoid rewriting same-level data, cutting write amplification 2.4–3× and raising write throughput 6.7× vs RocksDB. |
| Dostoevsky: Better Space-Time Trade-Offs for LSM-Tree Based Key-Value Stores via Adaptive Removal of Superfluous Merging | ★★★☆☆ | https://nivdayan.github.io/dostoevsky.pdf | Dayan et al. | 2018-06-01 | 2026-11-06 | Introduced Lazy Leveling and the Fluid LSM-tree, removing merges from all but the largest level to dominate prior designs on space-time trade-offs. |
| Redesigning LSMs for Nonvolatile Memory with NoveLSM | ★★★☆☆ | https://www.usenix.org/conference/atc18/presentation/kannan | Kannan et al. | 2018-07-01 | 2026-11-06 | Redesigned LevelDB for byte-addressable NVM with a mutable persistent skip-list memtable, cutting write latency up to 3.8× and speeding recovery. |
| SILK: Preventing Latency Spikes in Log-Structured Merge Key-Value Stores | ★★★☆☆ | https://www.usenix.org/conference/atc19/presentation/balmau | Balmau et al. | 2019-07-01 | 2026-11-06 | Traced LSM tail latency to interference between client writes, flushes and compactions and added an I/O scheduler (bandwidth allocation, prioritisation, preemption) cutting p99 latency up to 100×. |
| LSM-Based Storage Techniques: A Survey | ★★★☆☆ | [1812.07527](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.07527) | Luo et al. | 2019-07-01 | 2026-11-06 | Taxonomy and survey of LSM improvements and of the storage layers of LevelDB, RocksDB, HBase, Cassandra and AsterixDB, the standard LSM literature map. |
| From WiscKey to Bourbon: A Learned Index for Log-Structured Merge Trees | ★★★☆☆ | https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi20/presentation/dai | Dai et al. | 2020-11-01 | 2026-11-06 | First learned index inside a production LSM (WiscKey), using greedy piecewise-linear regression over immutable SSTables with a cost-benefit learning policy, improving lookups 1.23–1.78×. |


Note: LevelDB and BadgerDB have no peer-reviewed paper; their rows are system entries with primary-source links, included because the systems are canonical. RocksDB likewise has no paper of its own — covered by the Dong et al. (CIDR 2017) and MyRocks rows.
